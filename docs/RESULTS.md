# Leg 1 Empirical Results: The Entanglement Bottleneck

## Executive Summary
The first leg of the SPECTRA-CQ research sought to benchmark the efficacy of Variational Quantum Circuits (VQCs) for mapping structural degradation using 2D continuous energy spectrograms. 

By executing the pipeline through classical baselines and a rigorous ablation study on both quantum topology and circuit depth, we established a hard boundary condition: **Naive global entanglement natively triggers catastrophic gradient vanishing (Barren Plateaus) when processing continuous macroscopic structural data, regardless of circuit depth.**

## Phase 2: The Classical Baseline (CAE)
To establish a definitive target for "quantum advantage", a purely Classical Convolutional Autoencoder (CAE) was trained on the 2D energy density matrices. 
* **Protocol:** 15-Epoch constraint across 3 deterministic seeds (42, 100, 2026).
* **Result:** The CAE converged rapidly and exhibited high stability invariant of seed initialization.
* **Benchmark MSE:** The final Validation MSE converged tightly between `0.0087` and `0.0089`.

## Phase 3: The Hybrid Quantum Baseline (HQAE)
The 8-dimensional classical bottleneck was replaced with a PennyLane VQC utilizing 3 layers of `BasicEntanglerLayers` (local ring topology) to test if quantum spatial coupling offered an advantage over the classical baseline.
* **Protocol:** Exact same convolutional layers, 15-Epoch constraint, and deterministic seeds as the classical baseline.
* **Result:** The gradients vanished immediately. Across all three deterministic seeds, the model became permanently trapped in a Barren Plateau by Epoch 2.
* **Benchmark MSE:** Flatlined at a Validation MSE of `~0.0319`.

## Phase 4a Ablation: Entanglement Topology (Depth Locked at 3)
To isolate why the Phase 3 baseline failed, we tested three distinct quantum topologies:
1. **Zero Entanglement (`none`):** 
    * Qubits operated as independent rotations (control).
    * Exhibited high variance ("initialization roulette").
    * But, was the only quantum configuration capable of learning, reaching a Validation MSE of `~0.010`.
2. **Local Entanglement (`basic`):** 
    * Ring topology (Phase 3 Baseline). 
    * Flatlined at Validation MSE `~0.0319`.
3. **Global Entanglement (`strong`):** 
    * All-to-all topology. 
    * Gradients vanished immediately across all seeds, flatlining at MSE `~0.0317`.

*Insight:* Introducing `CNOT` gates to force spatial coupling created a noise landscape that completely blinded the classical Adam optimizer.

## Phase 4b Ablation: Circuit Depth (Topology Locked at `strong`)
To test if the Barren Plateau observed in 4a was strictly a symptom of over-parameterization, we ablated the circuit depth using the global topology:
* **Depth 1 (Shallow):** Flatlined at a Validation MSE of `~0.0317`.
* **Depth 3 (Intermediate):** Flatlined at a Validation MSE of `~0.0317`.
* **Depth 5 (Deep):** Flatlined at a Validation MSE of `~0.0317`.

*Insight:* The Barren Plateau is topology-dependent, not solely depth-dependent. Even at the absolute minimum depth of 1 layer, the quantum noise generated by generalized 3D Euler rotations and global entanglement overwhelms the loss landscape.

## Conclusion & Leg 2 Roadmap
Currently, classical convolutional architectures (or strictly unentangled quantum hybrid models) remain superior for the stable, high-fidelity compression of vibrational strain data. 

**Next Steps (Leg 2):** To unlock the theoretical advantages of globally entangled HQAEs for structural health monitoring, Leg 2 of this research will focus on breaking the Barren Plateau. We will optimize the quantum architecture by moving away from standard global cost functions and implementing advanced NISQ-era mitigation protocols, specifically evaluating **Localized Cost Functions** and **Layer-wise Training**.
